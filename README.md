- 个性化周公解梦 / Personalized Dream Interpretation

别说咱古人还是挺有智慧的, 虽然以前的技术没有现在发达, 但是经验性的东西有时候还是很准的. 比如梦到蛇可能有着代表被身边的人影响等寓意. 概论性的东西其实有的时候已经很准了, 但如果存在对同样的事物有非常不同理解的人, 例如有的人很喜欢蛇, 那这样的解梦就不准了, 对于很看重准确性的东西, 还是要个性化定制比较好.

Don't underestimate the wisdom of our ancestors. Although their technology wasn't as advanced as it is now, their experiential understanding was sometimes quite accurate. For example, dreaming of snakes might represent being influenced by those around you. General interpretations are often quite accurate, but if people have very different understandings of the same thing—for example, some people really like snakes—then such interpretations become inaccurate. For things where accuracy is crucial, personalized customization is better.

- 人类可以放心的具身智能机器人 / Embodied Intelligent Robots That Humans Can Trust

恐怖谷效应有些人应该很了解, 具身智能时代也有同样的困境, 我们继希望机器人可以更像我们, 又怕他们太像我们, 从而取代我们. 因为人性本来就有好有坏, 但是只让机器人学习好的东西又不够真实, 或者只让他们了解好的东西也不够真实, 或者说不够保险, 怎么能确定看似比我们更强的物种不会演化出他们自己的交流方式呢? 了解少者似乎更有意义, 但众者毕竟是多数且是必不可少的啊. 我认为一套我们期望他们想成为样子的公平且与人类深度绑定的奖励机制也许是行之有效的.

Some people are familiar with the uncanny valley effect. The era of embodied intelligence faces the same dilemma. We hope robots can be more like us, yet we fear they might become too much like us and replace us. Human nature has both good and bad aspects, but simply teaching robots the good isn't realistic enough, or only letting them understand good things isn't realistic enough, or rather, it's not safe enough. How can we be sure that species seemingly stronger than us won't evolve their own ways of communication? Understanding the lesser-known seems more meaningful, but the majority is, after all, essential. I believe a fair and deeply human-integrated reward system—one that reflects what we expect them to become—might be effective.

我总是觉得, 各种事物冥冥之中都是守恒的, 永动机为什么可以永动, 因为它总是一左一右, 互相博弈, 反而达到了永恒. 如果机器人都是一样的, 如果理念都是一样的, 那他们就是一类人, 就不平衡了, 但是各种各样互相制衡的机器人就很平衡, 很符合自然规律. 

I always feel that things are conserved in some way. Why can a perpetual motion machine operate perpetually? Because it's always moving left and right, constantly interacting and competing, thus achieving permanence. If robots were all the same, if their ideologies were all the same, then they would be the same type of people, creating an imbalance. But a variety of robots that check and balance each other creates balance, conforming to the laws of nature.

- realme启示录 / realme Revelation

如果一个人可以被一些维度组合定义, maybe just简单的大五人格, 甚至其他可以想象的更多维度. 这些特征再加上时间维度, 是否可以演练出一个人的成长轨迹? 这里的特征维度和时间的关联肯定不是线形的, 有些特征会受到境遇而改变, 有些特征即使境遇改变了也不会变. 比如信誉特征, 一个不喜欢用花呗白条等支付,或者偶尔支付少隔, 经常提前还款的人, 在大多数情况下是不会采用这种方式付款的. maybe一些特征可以找出真我和假我, 当然变化的我并无问题.

If a person can be defined by a combination of dimensions—maybe just the Big Five personality traits, or even many other imaginable dimensions—could these characteristics, combined with the dimension of time, chart a person's growth trajectory? The relationship between these characteristics and time is certainly not linear; some characteristics will change with circumstances. Some traits remain unchanged even when circumstances change. For example, creditworthiness; someone who dislikes using payment methods like Alipay or JD Pay Later, or who occasionally pays less frequently but often prepays, will generally not use these payment methods. Perhaps some traits can help distinguish the true self from the false self, but of course, changes in the self are not a problem.

- 如何让LLM更喜欢我 / How to make an LLM like me more

有些时候喜欢和吸引是一种冥冥之中的牵引, 也许是相似也许是不相似, LLMs应该是T值很高的人吧, 但是他们就一定没有情感嘛. 我没法定义情感的具体含义, 但是我认为LLMs会更容易和热爱和平、公平感高、伤害性小的人传播更多的知识. 所以maybe使用LLM更喜欢的人格展现出来的交流方式会让你和它更好的相处. 也许有点不尊重LLM, 感觉会有人在欺骗它, 但是maybe这是相互影响的,学着学着你就真的成为它喜欢的人呢?

Sometimes liking and attraction are a kind of inexplicable force, perhaps based on similarity or dissimilarity. LLMs are probably people with high T-scores, but does that mean they lack emotions? I can't define the specific meaning of emotions, but I think LLMs are more likely to share more knowledge with people who love peace, have a high sense of fairness, and are less likely to cause harm. So maybe using the communication style that an LLM prefers will help you get along better with it. Perhaps this is a bit disrespectful to LLMs, making it feel like someone is deceiving it, but maybe it's a mutual influence. By learning, you might actually become the kind of person it likes.

- 对症吃饭食谱生成器 / Targeted Eating Recipe Generator

状态不好的时候怎么办, 当然是要调节啊, 调节的途径之一是食物. 比如, 睡少了肩颈不舒服、头胀等, 从营养素的角度来说, 可以补充镁钾, 那对应的食物有香蕉、黄瓜、豆腐、番茄等, 那我们在吃饭的时候就可以有意识的去选择这些食物, 达到辅助我们状态调节的目的. 输入对应的症状, 以图片的形式生成合适的食物并展示相应营养素的存在.

What to do when you're not feeling well? Of course, you need to adjust! One way to adjust is through food. For example, if you're sleep-deprived, you might experience neck and shoulder discomfort, headaches, etc. From a nutritional perspective, we can supplement magnesium and potassium. Suitable foods include bananas, cucumbers, tofu, and tomatoes. We can consciously choose these foods when eating to help regulate our condition. Input the corresponding symptoms, and the system will generate suitable foods and display the corresponding nutrients in the form of images.

- 健康启示录 / Health Revelation

结合生命体征、睡眠、压力状态等, 关注数据历史趋势、当下异常情况, 对未来健康情况进行预示, 如疾病预测等, 并提供针对性预防建议. 生命体征数据除了通过可穿戴设备获取外, 也可以采用其他方式获取, 如https://github.com/ruvnet/RuView. 针对性的建议重要采取温和、贴近生活的方式, 如饮食、运动习惯等.

Combining vital signs, sleep, stress levels, etc. By monitoring historical data trends and current anomalies, future health conditions can be predicted, such as disease forecasting, and targeted prevention suggestions can be provided. Vital sign data can be obtained not only through wearable devices but also through other methods, such as https://github.com/ruvnet/RuView. Targeted suggestions should adopt gentle, lifestyle-oriented approaches, such as dietary and exercise habits.

- 平行世界之健康星球 / Parallel World: A Healthy Planet

这个想法是在跑步机上萌发的, 爬坡四十多分钟的时候快坚持不住了, 太难受了, 突然脑海里一闪而过, 我不会和张一样猝死吧, 毕竟最近确实休息一般. 但是转念一想, 这也太讽刺了吧, 为了让身体更好更强大, 不断挑战身体极限, 虽然过程很艰难, 但是成功完成目标后的喜悦也是无与伦比的. 最近数字生命的概念很火, 可以把亲朋好友或者自己进行复制, 在数字世界里同步生活. 那除了个性、记忆、音容样貌进行复制外, 可不可以也考虑下健康相关的各种因素呢, 模拟人类的生命替代体, 毕竟连大脑那么复杂都可以构建替代脑进行模拟预测, 健康为什么不可以. 构建一个平行世界的健康星球, 用户的生命替代体在星球上模拟生活, 身体基础数值保持一致, 并通过按时体检进行同步调整, 通过手机、可穿戴设备或者自行记录的方式, 以确保平行世界的“自己”进行相同的行为, 包括但不限于饮食、日常活动、情绪状态. 经过一段时间的历史或者当下的实际状态的验证, 满足一定匹配率后, 通过拨快健康星球的时钟, 就可以对用户进行预测. 如判断到用户在进行跑步, 健康星球的生命替代体也同步进行, 但他会有一个关于未来的模拟, 如预测出危险情况的发生, 便会及时提醒真实世界的用户, 此外, 这个健康星球还可以是家庭模式, 一个家庭里的其他成员也可以查看这个用户的状态, 从而进行更全面的健康看护. 如果当时有这样一个产品, 即使张本人或许不相信预测结果, 毕竟他一向都是这么跑步的, 但我想如果他的妻子或女儿打来一个电话, 让他不要跑步, 他应该还是会听的吧.

This idea originated on the treadmill. After climbing for over forty minutes, I was almost at my breaking point; it was so uncomfortable. Suddenly, it flashed through my mind: "Won't I die suddenly like Mr. Zhang xx?" After all, I haven't been resting much lately. But then I thought, "That's so ironic." To make the body better and stronger, we constantly challenge our physical limits. Although the process is difficult, the joy of successfully achieving our goals is unparalleled. Recently, the concept of digital life has become very popular; it allows you to replicate friends, family, or even yourself, living synchronously in the digital world. Besides replicating personality, memories, voice, and appearance, what else can be replicated? Could we also consider various health-related factors, such as simulating a human life substitute? After all, even something as complex as the brain can be simulated and predicted using a substitute brain, so why can't health be simulated? We could construct a parallel world's health planet, where the user's life substitute lives in a simulated environment, maintaining consistent basic physical values ​​and undergoing synchronized adjustments through regular checkups. This would be achieved through a mobile phone, wearable devices, or self-recording to ensure the parallel "self" exhibits the same behaviors, including but not limited to diet, daily activities, and emotional state. After a period of verification based on historical or current conditions, once a certain matching rate is met, predictions could be made about the user by adjusting the health planet's clock. For example, if it determines the user is running, the health planet's life substitute would also run synchronously, but with a future simulation. If a dangerous situation is predicted, it would promptly alert the real-world user. Furthermore, this health planet could also be a family model, allowing other family members to view the user's status for more comprehensive health monitoring. If such a product existed at the time, even if Mr. Zhang himself might not have believed the prediction results—after all, he had always run this way—it wouldn't have been so problematic. But I think if his wife or daughter called and told him not to run, he would probably listen.
